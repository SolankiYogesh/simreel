Use your phone’s camera in the iOS Simulator

Test with real faces, documents and codes without leaving the Simulator. SimReel streams your phone’s camera to your Mac over Wi-Fi — the phone only needs a browser.

What you need

  • SimReel installed on your Mac (macOS 14+, Apple Silicon, Xcode with the iOS Simulator).
  • A phone with a current browser — Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android.
  • Both on the same Wi-Fi network. Guest or isolated networks that block devices from talking to each other won’t work.

Connect your phone

  1. Open SimReel and choose the Wi-Fi camera source, then press Turn on phone cameras.
  2. Scan the QR code with your phone’s camera app and open the link.
  3. The first time, the phone warns that the connection isn’t private. That’s because your Mac uses its own certificate. Tap Show Details and visit the website (on Android: Advanced, then Proceed). Browsers require HTTPS for camera access, which is why the certificate exists.
  4. On the page, tap Start camera and allow camera access. Choose the back or front camera and the quality.
  5. Your phone appears in the Phones list in SimReel. Select it — your simulator apps now receive its camera.

macOS may ask whether SimReel can accept incoming network connections. Choose Allow. Keep the phone page open and the screen on while streaming.

From the command line

simreel phone on        # starts the listener and prints the link
simreel devices         # phones that have connected
simreel set-source --remote "iPhone"
simreel phone off

Is it safe?

  • The listener is off by default and stops when you turn it off or quit SimReel.
  • Every link includes a random token that changes each time you turn it on; requests without it are refused.
  • Frames travel only to your Mac, and nothing is sent to the internet.

Portrait and landscape

Hold the phone upright and the Simulator gets portrait frames; turn it sideways and it gets landscape. Portrait sources are not cropped to landscape.

Troubleshooting

  • The phone can’t open the link. Check both devices are on the same network, phone cameras are on in SimReel, and you allowed incoming connections.
  • The camera won’t start. Use Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android), accept the certificate warning and allow camera access.
  • Choppy video. Pick Light (480p) on the phone page, or move closer to the router.
  • Apps still show no camera. Make sure the simulator shows Camera active in SimReel and relaunch your app.

This feature has been verified with a simulated phone. Real iPhone and Android browsers are expected to work — please report what you find. For a broader introduction, see how to use a camera in the iOS Simulator.
